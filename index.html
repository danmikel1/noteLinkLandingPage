What It Does

A Rich Feature Set

More than just note-taking — a real-time platform for capturing, organizing, and collaborating on your ideas anywhere.

Secure Authentication

Full registration and login powered by Firebase Auth, including session persistence and password reset.

Real-Time Collaboration

Invite others to view or edit your notes simultaneously. Presence indicators show who's active right now.

Integrated Chat

Chat with collaborators directly in the app. Dedicated chat rooms per note with live unread badge counts.

Powerful Search

Instantly find notes by scanning titles and full content across your entire library.

Peer-to-Peer Sharing

Share individual notes with specific users and control view or edit permissions with ease.

Note Recovery

Accidentally deleted something? The bin lets you view and restore any deleted note.
